# Build → Portfolio + Content + Product

One build, three outputs. Write in simple, plain English. Ken's own voice: practical, honest, a Filipino freelancer learning in public.

## Inputs
1. The build folder or files (in `artifacts/` or the module folder)
2. `data/session_log.csv` rows for this module (real struggles = good content)
3. `learning/LEARNER_STATE.md` (Assessor notes)

## Step 1 — Portfolio case study
Use `artifacts/portfolio_template.md`. Save as `artifacts/{module}_{short-name}.md`.

**Check before saving:**
- No real client data, names, or numbers. Only fake/practice data.
- Every claim has proof: a number, a screenshot path, or a test result.
- "Lessons learned" includes at least one real mistake from the session log.

## Step 2 — Posts
Write 3 short posts into `content/posts/{date}_{short-name}.md`:
1. **Story post** — what I built and what went wrong first
2. **How-to post** — one useful step others can copy
3. **Before/after post** — time or error rate, manual vs automated

Rules:
- English by default. Ken may ask for a Taglish version.
- No hype words ("game-changer", "revolutionary"). No income promises.
- End with a question to the reader, not a sales pitch.

## Step 3 — Product chapter draft
Write `product/chapters/{module}_{short-name}.md`:
- Who this is for
- What you'll be able to do
- Steps (numbered, short)
- Common mistakes (from the session log)
- Practice exercise with fake data
- Check: "You're done when..."

**Check:** Don't copy text from any course, brochure, or article. Use your own words and Ken's own builds.

## Step 4 — Report
Tell Ken in 3 lines what was created and where. Suggest one post to publish first.
