# Market Scan — 2026-10-03 (first scan, public sources only)

## Summary
1. Bookkeeping demand from abroad is large: 291 bookkeeper posts on onlinejobs.ph today; thousands on JobStreet PH (Xero, AU).
2. **US + QuickBooks Online** is the biggest lane. **AU + Xero** is second.
3. Top pain: **messy, behind books** (catch-up / cleanup), especially **construction** (job costing) and **e-commerce**.
4. Plain bookkeeping pay is low ($2.50–$15/hr). Sell automation + clean books, not hours.
5. New signal: **"AI-native" bookkeeping firms** now hire in PH (one AU, one US seen today).
