# JHU "AI and Agentic AI in Finance" — Notes (inspiration only)

> Summary in our own words. Do not copy the brochure's text or curriculum into the product.

## Facts
- 13-week online program, 6–8 h/week, ~100 learning hours, $2,900.
- Audience: enterprise finance (FP&A, risk, compliance, credit, investment).
- Tools named: Claude, Claude Code, Gemini Notebook. Claude API access not included.
- Cites a 2026 CFO Connect report: 56% of finance leaders use AI, 45% of teams stuck in pilot, 17% have AI in core workflows.

## What we take (methods)
1. Human-in-the-loop checkpoints
2. Data validation checklist (catch made-up numbers)
3. Client data safety (PII)
4. Prompt chaining with a check after each step
5. Audit logs for every AI decision
6. Testing AI output against known-correct answers
7. Cost per task
8. Build vs buy

## Structure we borrow
Pre-work → foundations → applied → **project** → **break** → advanced → governance → **capstone**.
(Our curriculum follows this: M0 → M1–M2 → M3–M4 → Week 8 break → M5 → M6.)

## Project idea that fits small clients
Invoice / expense checker against a written policy, with an audit log. → Our M4 build.

## What we skip
Credit underwriting, KYC/AML, fraud models, portfolio risk agents, earnings-call sentiment. Too enterprise.
